How to read this

One minimal, classy page for a new furniture business. Each page below is one section, with the phone and the desk built as two separate designs. Nothing here is the final look: it fixes the order, the words and where every picture goes.

Marks you will see

[Brand name] a dashed timber bracket is a placeholder for a real name, price or fact. None is invented.

A labelled ivory panel is a picture slot with its size. Furniture is never drawn here, only photographed later.
Three rules this page keeps

1. Two ways in: buy it ready or build it yours.
2. Phone is centred and clear of the island and home bar. Desk starts at the left gutter.
3. Every button ends in WhatsApp with the choices already in the message.

Next steps
Stop here
Nothing built, nothing deployed
What happens next

1. You paste CHATGPT-THREE-LOOKS.md into ChatGPT with this storyboard attached. It returns three looks. You pick one.

2. You make the stand-in pictures with STAND-IN-IMAGES.md. The first six are essential.

3. Chat 2 builds the page in the look you chose. Chat 3 runs the gates (phone view, desk view, every button pressed, speed).

4. Nothing goes live until the client confirms the name, the pieces, the prices and the photos.

Needed from you or the client

1. Does the client want a language switch (English, Sinhala) in the header?
2. Which one piece leads the hero? It decides the photo and the drawing.
3. Which four rooms does the showroom or catalogue cover?
4. How many pieces are ready to buy today? What is the delivery promise?
5. Which sizes, fabrics and presets are real? What does each change in the price?
6. Which three details prove the quality?
7. Is it made by hand in Sri Lanka? Which timber? Only then can we write a line here.
8. Is there a showroom? If not, this headline changes to a delivery line.

Honest limits

Every product, price and photo here is a placeholder. AI pictures are stand-ins for a first look and never go live on a client's address. ChatGPT rarely holds one camera spot across four pictures, so the room set may need a second try.
