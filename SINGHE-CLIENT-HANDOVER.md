# Singhe Furniture — client-specific concept

This GitHub demo is a Business Booster design proposal. It is not the official Singhe website and does not replace it.

## Implemented
- Singhe name and heritage-led hero: Rooted in teak. Made for your life.
- New architectural hero image, optional timber/joinery/finished views, native scrolling.
- Six links to official product categories.
- Existing interactive sofa builder and four multi-finish product previews retained.
- Actual published central telephone, Moratuwa address, opening hours, and official showroom link.
- Noindex on the proposal. Generated imagery and sample dimensions remain disclosed.
